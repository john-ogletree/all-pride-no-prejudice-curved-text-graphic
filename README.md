## 📐 Overview & Concept

This repository exists strictly as a visual design sample and portfolio piece. Originally conceived as an apparel graphic for hoodies and t-shirts, the layout features repeating wavy text in a Pride rainbow color palette alongside complementary line art elements.

* **Original Context:** Apparel Design Graphic (Hoodie / T-Shirt Print)
* **Project Purpose:** Portfolio Piece / Concept Art
* **GitHub Upload Date:** August 10, 2026
* **Asset File:** `Colorful Lines Cute & Cheeky Curved Text Hoodie.png`
* **Format:** Landscape High-Resolution PNG Image

## 🔍 Design Breakdown

* **Typography & Layout:**
  * **Main Phrase:** `ALL PRIDE, NO PREJUDICE` stacked in repeating wave-curved rows
  * **Lettering Style:** Bold, rounded sans-serif typeface following fluid, undulating baseline paths
* **Visual Elements:**
  * Arch line art motifs framing the left and right sides of the typographic stack
  * Scattered small heart vector accents surrounding the curved text
* **Color Palette:** Six-tier Pride spectrum featuring Crimson Red, Vibrant Orange, Golden Yellow, Emerald Green, Cyan Blue, and Deep Purple on a Crisp White background
